Publications and Preprints

All works listed here were developed independently by the author, without institutional affiliation or external funding. Unless otherwise noted, these papers are theoretical frameworks rather than empirical or algorithmic implementations.

Unified Mastery Theory (UMT) Ecosystem

These works together form a unified theoretical system concerned with mastery, learning, evaluation, epistemic limits, and governance of inference.

  • Unified Mastery Theory (UMT): Axiomatic Foundations and Governance Structure
    Canonical consolidation document defining scope, axioms, admissibility, scalability, and governance of the UMT ecosystem.
  • Unified Mastery Meta-Theory (UMMT)
    Axiomatic meta-theory defining mastery as a latent, multidimensional equilibrium state under identifiability and validity constraints.

Core Theoretical Frameworks

Foundational frameworks derived from or operating under the UMMT axioms.

  • Conceptual Mastery Evaluation Systems (CMES)
  • Mastery Equilibrium Theory (MET)
  • Functional Equivalence Class Hypothesis (FECH)
  • Counterfactual Predictive Sufficiency (CPS)
  • Reconstruction Lower Bounds for Learner Models (RLB-LM)
  • Taxonomy of Mastery Failure Modes (TMF)
  • Mastery Minimax Principle (MMP)
  • Construct Validity Framework (CVF)

Derived and Extended Frameworks

  • Epistemic Adversarial Pressure (EAP) and Adversarial Inference Regimes (AIR)
  • Mastery Lifecycle Initialization (MLI)

Related Theoretical Work
