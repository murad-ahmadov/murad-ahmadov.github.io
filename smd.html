Systemic Mastery Density (SMD)

Systemic Mastery Density (SMD) extends mastery analysis from individual agents to institutional and multi-agent systems. The framework studies how structural competence is distributed, concentrated, or diluted within organizations and evaluation regimes.

SMD introduces density-based metrics for systemic capability, identifying how evaluation structures, governance constraints, and information bottlenecks affect the effective mastery capacity of a system.

Core Themes

  • Distributed structural competence
  • Institutional evaluation constraints
  • System-level identifiability limits
  • Governance and epistemic fragility

Status

Active theoretical framework under continued development. See the Publications page for associated preprints.
