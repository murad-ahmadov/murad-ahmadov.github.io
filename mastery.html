Mastery and Evaluation Frameworks

This research group develops formal theoretical frameworks for analyzing mastery, evaluation systems, and the limits of structural inference in educational, cognitive, and artificial domains.

The central concern is the distinction between observable performance and latent structural competence, and the conditions under which claims of mastery are justified or underdetermined.

Frameworks

  • Unified Mastery Theory (UMT)
  • Systemic Mastery Density (SMD)
  • Epistemic Trajectory Framework
